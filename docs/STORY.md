# SMALL PARTS story bible

One voice for every line in the game: cutscenes, walkie-talkie calls, banners, level cards, loading tips, the death screen. If a line is not in the spirit of this page, it does not go in.

## Tone

Late night hackerspace comedy. Two friends, a building full of tools, and a problem nobody planned for. Nobody panics. Disasters get the voice of a shop rule taped to a machine ("PLEASE RETURN THE SCALE TO 1:1 WHEN YOU ARE DONE"). The jokes come from treating a war against five hundred plastic army men as a cleanup job, and from the lab's own culture: do-ocracy, label everything, clean your bench, leave it better than you found it. Affectionate, never mean. No gore: army men fall over, melt a little, and lie still. No swearing, no real brands, no winking at other games.

## Cast

- **MOHEEB.** Builder, tinkerer, always mid-project. Talks fast, makes plans out loud, improvises weapons from whatever is in reach. Optimistic about everything, including things that are on fire.
- **LUIS.** Steady, dry, practical. Reads the manual, then ignores it for good reasons. Undercuts Moheeb's plans with one line, then helps carry them out.
- Whichever one the player does not pick stays at the other's side as a buddy, fighting and talking. Cutscenes show both.
- **The Army.** Five hundred green plastic army men from a print job someone left queued for a kids' class: `ARMY_MEN_x500.gcode`. Brought to life by the same pulse that shrank the heroes. They are army men, so they do what army men do: take ground and hold it. They salute, march in step and shout orders in tiny voices. Tan figures are the veterans.
- **THE SERGEANT.** A tan figure printed at double size, with a megaphone. Runs the woodshop as a forward base. Mid boss.
- **THE GENERAL.** The display figure from the hacker window: a twelve-inch action figure with a talking pull-string box, now in charge. Final boss. He speaks in recorded phrases ("HOLD THE LINE." "PULL MY STRING." "THIS IS MY BENCH NOW.").
- **THE LAB.** HeatSync Labs at 108 West Main, after hours, lights low, machines ticking as they cool. Every area is the real one: the lasers, the print farm, the craft tables, sewing, member storage, coworking, the lounge, electronics, the woodshop, the machine shop.

## The premise

After open hack night, Moheeb and Luis stay late to test a project in the laser corner: a laser cutter someone reflashed with firmware from a forum, which now has a setting called SCALE. Luis reads the label. Moheeb presses the button. The pulse shrinks both of them to 1:20, nine centimetres tall, and the same pulse runs through the print farm, where five hundred freshly printed army men stand up and look around.

The way back: the laser's settings reset when the lab's power cycles. The main breaker panel is in the machine shop at the back of the building. They have to cross the whole lab at nine centimetres tall, through an army that has decided the lab is a battlefield.

They are not unarmed. A hackerspace at toy scale is an armoury: a laser-cut rubber band repeater from the scrap bin, a hot glue gun, a coilgun off a capacitor bank, a soldering iron, LiPo packs that puff and pop, and a tiny drone's motors strapped on as jetpacks.

## Levels and beats

**Prologue (cutscene, before level 1).** The lab at night, full size. Moheeb and Luis at the laser corner. The SCALE button. A flash. The camera falls to the floor and finds them, tiny, on the laser bed. Across the room, a print farm full of little green men sits up. Moheeb: "Okay. New project." Luis: "We are the project."

**Level 1, LASER CORNER.** Tutorial: move, jump, the jetpack (a tiny drone's motors, stripped in the prologue), the rubber band repeater. First army patrol. Walkie beats:
- Landing on the laser bed: "Mind the honeycomb."
- The scrap bin: they find the rubber band repeater, laser-cut, slightly charred.
- First contact: a squad of army men marches in step. "They're in formation." "They came out of the printer in formation."
- Exit: down off the laser table and into the aisle toward member storage.

**Level 2, MEMBER STORAGE.** The storage bins and sewing wall, where the army has set up a supply line. The hot glue gun is in someone's labelled bin. Beats:
- The bins: every one labelled with a member's name. "We are not opening anyone's bin." "We are opening this one. It says GLUE."
- Sewing: thread spools as cover, pins in a cushion. "Don't land on the cushion."
- Exit: the art and LEGO wall, the army's lookout towers.

**Level 3, PRINT FARM.** The print farm and craft tables, where the army is still being printed. Objective: unplug the four printers at the power strip on the floor by the rack (or shoot them). Beats:
- The printers' screens read ARMY_MEN_x500, 61 PERCENT. "It's still printing them." "Then we stop the print."
- Each printer down: its plug comes out of the strip, its screen goes dark, a banner, PRINT CANCELLED.
- The LEGO towers: tan veterans on the battlements.
- Exit: the job queue is empty. The army is not. Out to the front windows.

**Level 4, WINDOW DISPLAYS.** The storefront: the gallery bay, the robot counter with its LED cube, the hacker bay with its CRT wall and twenty three rubber ducks. Luis clipped a printer gantry on the way out, so the jetpack props are chewed; the robots have spares. Objective: find four spare props. Beats:
- The ducks: "They've drafted the ducks."
- The LED cube: somebody's thesis. Do not touch.
- Exit: past the counter and back to coworking.

**Level 5, COWORKING AND LOUNGE.** Under the desks, across the chairs, the couch as a fortress. The coilgun from the electronics side arrives here. Beats:
- Under the coworking tables: cables like jungle vines.
- The lounge: the army has the couch. "They have the high ground." "It's a couch."
- The TV wall and the bike bench.
- Exit: the electronics workstation, where the coilgun is waiting.

**Level 6, ELECTRONICS AND WOODSHOP.** The parts drawers, the soldering station, then the woodshop, where THE SERGEANT runs a forward base on the benches. The soldering iron melee. Mid boss: the Sergeant (short cutscene). Beats:
- Parts drawers: "Resistors. Ten thousand of them. Sorted."
- The soldering station: "Hot end first."
- The woodshop: sawdust drifts, the Sergeant's megaphone.
- Exit: the machine shop door is blocked (the army stacked the scrap bin against it), so they go the long way round, into the back hall.

**Level 7, KITCHEN AND DARKROOM.** The back hall, the supply closet, the biolab. The jetpack batteries will not make the climb to the breaker panel; there is a charger on the kitchen table, but its battery pack is gone. Little boot prints lead through the propped supply closet door to the hatch in its floor, the steel plate shoved aside, and down into the basement: dirt, drain pipes, no stairs, and the army's hideout. Down for the pack, up an old steel shelving unit dumped under the hatch and out on the jetpack, then the kitchen: plug in the charger and hold the room for sixty seconds. Beats:
- The supply closet: paper towels for a thousand years.
- The basement: "Dirt, drain pipes and a lot of army. No stairs."
- The fridge: somebody labelled this yogurt in twenty nineteen.
- Exit: full charge.

**Level 8, METAL FAB BAY.** Welders, the weld table, an anvil, and the army's supply dump: five crate stacks of spare army men. Objective: wreck the dump (shoot it or throw a LiPo). Beats:
- "That one was all left boots."
- "Do not get under the anvil."
- Exit: the connecting door into the machine shop.

**Level 9, MACHINE SHOP.** The lathe, the mills, the breaker panel on the far wall. THE GENERAL waits on the bench in front of it (boss reveal cutscene): "THIS IS MY BENCH NOW." His weak points: the pull-string box on his back, the battery hatch, the two knee joints. Moheeb: "His battery hatch has a screw missing." Luis: "Everyone's does."

**Ending (cutscene).** The General powers down mid-phrase. They jetpack up to the breaker panel and flip the main. The lights go out. The lights come back. Two full-size people sit on the machine shop floor among five hundred very still army men. Moheeb puts one in his pocket. Luis finds a note taped to the laser: PLEASE RETURN THE SCALE TO 1:1 WHEN YOU ARE DONE. Credits. After them, the print farm screen: ARMY_MEN_x500, 62 PERCENT.

## Writing rules

- Lines are short: at most two lines of caption, about 90 characters each.
- Speakers in captions: "MOHEEB", "LUIS", "THE SERGEANT", "THE GENERAL", "ARMY MAN".
- No emdashes, no double dashes, no emoji.
- No real brand names on machines or parts. The lab's own name and address are fine.
- Never mean about real people. Moheeb and Luis are the heroes.
